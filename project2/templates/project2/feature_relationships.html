📈 PDP vs ALE under Feature Relationships

Why compare both?

PDP evaluates the selected feature across its range while retaining the other observed feature values.

When features are related, this can evaluate combinations that are uncommon in the dataset.

ALE instead evaluates local changes within intervals where observations actually occur. It is therefore generally less affected by correlated features, although it does not remove every possible dependency issue.

👤 Human-Centric Takeaway

Feature-effect plots should not be interpreted without considering the data on which they are based.

Showing feature relationships alongside PDP and ALE helps the user recognise when an explanation may rely on unusual combinations of feature values.

This makes limitations of the explanation visible rather than presenting a plot as an unquestionable explanation of model behaviour.
