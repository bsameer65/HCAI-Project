This app trains and explains machine learning models on the Palmer Penguins dataset. It is designed to help you understand why a model makes a prediction, not just what it predicts.

📋 Dataset Overview

Features are the measurements we give to the model — they are the clues the model uses to make a decision. The target is what the model is trying to predict.

In this project, the model looks at penguin measurements (features) and predicts which species the penguin belongs to (target).
