Project 2: Explainability

This app trains and explains machine learning models on the Palmer Penguins dataset. It is designed to help beginners understand why a model makes a prediction, not just what it predicts.

Home

About the Dataset

The Palmer Penguins dataset contains measurements of 344 penguins from three species — Adelie, Chinstrap, and Gentoo — collected at the Palmer Station in Antarctica.

We use the following features to predict species:

  • bill_length_mm – length of the penguin's bill
  • bill_depth_mm – depth of the penguin's bill
  • flipper_length_mm – length of the flipper
  • body_mass_g – body mass in grams
  • island – island where the penguin was found (Biscoe, Dream, Torgersen)
  • sex – male or female
