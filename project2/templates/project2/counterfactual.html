What is a counterfactual explanation?
A counterfactual is an artificial penguin example with small, realistic changes to features — just enough to flip the model's prediction to your chosen species. It answers the question: "What would need to change for this penguin to be classified differently?"

Counterfactuals are ranked by how similar they are to the original penguin. The closest ones are the most useful — they show the minimum change needed to change the prediction.
