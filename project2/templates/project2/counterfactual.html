What are counterfactuals?
These are artificial penguin examples with small, realistic changes to features that are enough to flip the model's prediction to your chosen species. They answer the question: "What would need to change for this penguin to be classified differently?"

These counterfactuals show small realistic changes that would make the selected model predict another species. They are ranked by how much they differ from the original.
