Why Compare These Methods?

Standard ALE is model-agnostic. It estimates local effects by comparing predictions at the lower and upper boundaries of feature intervals.

Logistic Regression is differentiable, so its local probability sensitivity can also be calculated analytically from the fitted coefficients.

If both approaches produce similar accumulated effects, this provides additional evidence that the finite-difference ALE explanation is consistent with the model's analytical local behaviour.

Why not Decision Trees? Decision Trees are piecewise constant. Their predictions remain constant inside leaves and change abruptly at split thresholds, so a smooth derivative-based probability explanation is not appropriate.
