Method Agreement

i Why Logistic Regression only? Logistic Regression produces smooth probability functions whose derivatives can be calculated analytically. Decision Trees are piecewise constant: predictions remain unchanged inside leaves and change abruptly at split thresholds. A smooth derivative-based probability explanation is therefore not appropriate for Decision Trees.

Mean |Δ| measures the average absolute difference between the two ALE calculations. Maximum |Δ| reports the largest observed difference.
