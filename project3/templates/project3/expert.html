Why Simulate Experts?

Real expert labels are expensive and difficult to collect repeatedly. Simulated experts provide controlled and reproducible competence profiles, making it possible to measure whether a collaboration strategy discovers and uses expert strengths correctly.

The simulation does not assume that experts are always correct. Instead, each expert has a higher probability of being correct in selected regions of the input space and lower reliability elsewhere.
