What These Analyses Add

Complementarity

Measures whether the human knows things the AI does not, rather than only comparing individual accuracy.

Human workload

Coverage–accuracy analysis quantifies how much human effort is required to achieve a particular team performance.

Trustworthy confidence

Calibration tests whether model confidence is meaningful enough to support human-handoff decisions.

Robustness

Stability analysis distinguishes genuine effects from results that occur only under one random simulation.

Explanation through ablation

Removing information from the competence model shows which signals are actually useful for deciding when humans should intervene.

Label efficiency

Active-learning curves expose how quickly different strategies learn useful information as human queries increase.
