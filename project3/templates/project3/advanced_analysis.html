Advanced Human-AI Analysis

This analysis goes beyond individual model accuracy and investigates when human expertise complements the classifier, how much expert involvement is useful, whether classifier confidence is reliable, and how efficiently expert competence can be learned.

Advanced Analysis reuses results from the previous experiments rather than retraining the full system. This keeps the analysis consistent with the results reported on the other project pages.
