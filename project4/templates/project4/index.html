The comparison

Two interfaces, one preference model

Both methods provide evidence about which movie characteristics matter to a participant, but they ask for that evidence in different ways.

01
A / B

Pairwise choices

View two movies at a time and select the one you would rather watch. Each decision is quick and focused.

Two movies per task
02
1 — 10

Complete rankings

Arrange ten movies from most preferred to least preferred. One task gives a richer ordering but requires more thought.

Ten movies per task
About this project
